# Claims

## Allowed Claims

- This is a scaffolded local-only repo for public-safe synthetic energy studies.
- The repo documents assumptions, validation questions, and public/private/sealed boundaries.
- The first planned artifact is a synthetic solar-to-hydrogen control-ledger study.

## Forbidden Claims

- No deployed energy systems.
- No proven output.
- No certified safety.
- No commercial-readiness.
- No physical validation.
- No production CAD, production schematics, BOMs, Gerbers, exact dimensions, live operations, private sites, private facility layouts, sealed designs, or sealed geometry.
- No proof completion, release, or publication claims.
