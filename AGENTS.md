# AGENTS.md

This repo is a Phase 5 public scaffold for public-safe infrastructure energy studies.

## Operating Rules

- Keep all content synthetic, public-safe, and reviewable.
- Do not add deployed energy systems, proven output, certified safety, commercial-readiness, private measurements, customer data, Foundation-private data, production CAD, production schematics, BOMs, Gerbers, exact dimensions, live operations, private sites, private facility layouts, internal company product names, sealed designs, or sealed geometry.
- Keep status language limited to `planned`, `scaffolded`, `public scaffold`, `published scaffold`, and `not released` until human review approves any later release.
- Do not add remotes, push, publish, change metadata, or route this repo through `Franzabner` or `franzabner-proof-stack`.

## Done Means

Work is complete only when the requested files are present, public/private/sealed boundaries are explicit, validation passes, and review notes identify held items.
